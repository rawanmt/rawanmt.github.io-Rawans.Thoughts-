# The Person Behind the Thoughts

Hi, I'm Rawan!

I'm a cybersecurity professional with a background in digital forensics, an analytical mind, and a curiosity that rarely stops at the first answer.

## A Little About Me

I spend much of my time understanding how systems behave, investigating unusual activity, and connecting seemingly unrelated pieces of information.

What I enjoy most about cybersecurity isn't just the technology. It's the process of asking the right questions, challenging assumptions, and making sense of complicated situations.

But there's more to me than cybersecurity.

I'm naturally curious, independent in how I think, and always interested in exploring new ideas, perspectives, and experiences.

## What You'll Find Here

This blog is a collection of things that interest me.

Some posts will explore cybersecurity, digital investigations, technical challenges, and research.

Others might be personal observations, lessons I've learned, or thoughts that deserve more than a passing moment.

## Why Rawan's Thoughts?

Because I wanted a place where I could write without limiting myself to a single subject.

A place to learn in public, reflect, share ideas, and occasionally revisit what I thought I knew.

If something here makes you curious, teaches you something new, or gives you a different perspective, then this little corner of the internet has done its job.
